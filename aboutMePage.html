About Me

Here's what you should know about me:

I am an IT Programming student at Nova Scotia Community College. My studies have covered a variety of programming languages, software development, and web development.

One of my main projects is a Halifax Transit Android application built using Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. This project has given me practical experience developing an application and working with transit data.

I have also worked on Ticketbase using C# and HTML, along with other programming and web development projects. These projects have helped me develop my problem-solving and application development skills.

I particularly enjoy front-end development, especially building and customizing websites. However, I am also interested in back-end development and learning new technologies.

Outside of programming, I enjoy building and customizing computers, digital music production, and playing bass and electric guitar.
